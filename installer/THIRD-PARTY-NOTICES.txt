libmpv-2.dll
------------
VELA plays media through libmpv, the library form of the mpv media player
(https://mpv.io). The bundled build comes from
https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake (release 20261010,
mpv git b2c255c13e) and statically includes FFmpeg, libass, libplacebo and
other libraries.

This build of libmpv is licensed under the GNU General Public License,
version 2 or later. Source code for mpv is available at
https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv and the exact build recipe (including all
bundled libraries and versions) at
https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake.

FFmpeg   - https://ffmpeg.org        (GPL/LGPL; see https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html)
libass   - https://github.com/libass/libass (ISC)
libplacebo - https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libplacebo (LGPL-2.1+)
